About the Miagao

Welcome to Miagao, Iloilo, a beautiful municipality in the province of Iloilo, Philippines, known for its rich cultural heritage, historical landmarks, natural beauty, and warm hospitality. Miagao is home to the famous Miagao Church, a UNESCO World Heritage Site recognized for its unique Baroque architecture and historical significance. Beyond its historic landmarks, Miagao offers beautiful coastal areas, local delicacies, traditional products, and peaceful destinations that showcase the charm of the community. Our goal is to promote Miagao's tourism, culture, local businesses, and hidden gems. We aim to help visitors discover interesting places, explore local products, support small business owners, and appreciate the traditions and stories that make Miagao special. Through this platform, we hope to connect tourists, residents, and local entrepreneurs while encouraging sustainable tourism and supporting the growth of the local community. Discover Miagao. Experience its beauty. Celebrate its culture. Support its people.

Key Facts

  • Population: 68,163 residents (2024 population census).
  • Land Area: 156.80 square kilometers across 119 barangays.
  • Moniker: Known as the "Onion Capital of the Visayas".
  • Accessibility: About a 40-minute bus, jeepney, or van ride from Iloilo City.

Landmarks and Heritage

  • Miagao Church: Officially the Santo Tomás de Villanueva Parish Church, this 1797 fortress-church is a recognized UNESCO World Heritage Site famous for its intricate yellowish-brown sandstone facade and baroque architecture.
  • Salakayan Festival: Held annually during the first week of February to commemorate local resistance against historic pirate raids.
  • University of the Philippines Visayas (UPV): Hosts a major regional campus in town.
